AAXO is building optimisation intelligence for industrial facilities. We bring operational decisions and actions into a coordinated flow, with people setting the objectives and the limits.

### More from the plant you already run

Our focus spans **throughput, energy cost, quality, uptime and scheduling** — the decisions that connect one part of a facility to the next.

Start with one operating challenge, define what success looks like, and evaluate it through a scoped simulation or pilot.

### Human control, built in

Within AAXO's governed execution path, proposed actions are checked against policy, authority and current state. Operators review actions that require approval, and recorded outcomes provide a basis for inspection.

- **AAXO Runtime** governs execution, checks observed results and retains evidence.
- **AAXO Console** gives operators a place to supervise runs, review approvals and inspect what happened.
